I was listening to a podcast recently when an ad started playing. Something about the voice was just slightly off. I couldn't tell you what the ad was for, because I spent the whole time thinking: did a human lose a voice acting job? Could they not convince a person of this message? What else do they use AI for?

The book distinguishes three types of authenticity: representational (do your communications accurately depict your work?), relational (do your interactions feel personal and sincere?), and operational (do your practices match your stated values?). AI can threaten all three! But it can also support them, for example by freeing up staff time so they can invest time and effort in the personal, human interactions that build real trust.
